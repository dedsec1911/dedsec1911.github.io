Privacy Policy

How Max Player handles information when you use the app.

1. Overview

Max Player is a video player for Android. This policy explains what information the app accesses, stores, and shares. By using Max Player, you acknowledge the practices described here.

2. Media access

With your permission, Max Player reads video files and related media information from your device so it can display and play your library. The app can also open videos, streams, or subtitle files from locations and links you choose.

Max Player does not upload your video files, subtitle files, or media library to the developer’s servers. Streaming content is requested directly from the service or address you select, so that service may receive your IP address and request information under its own privacy policy.

3. Information stored on your device

To provide player features, Max Player stores the following information locally on your device:

  • video names, locations, duration, playback position, and playback history;
  • favorites, bookmarks, bookmark labels, and per-video settings;
  • selected subtitle locations and playback, subtitle, display, gesture, and library preferences; and
  • hidden-folder preferences.

This information is used only to operate the app’s library and playback features. You can remove history, favorites, and bookmarks from within the app where those controls are available, and you can remove all app data through your device’s Android settings.

4. Advertising

The free version of Max Player displays ads in library, folder, search, history, favorites, and settings screens. Ads are not shown in the video player.

Ads are provided by Google Mobile Ads. Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, ad interaction data, and technical information to deliver, personalize where permitted, measure, and protect advertising. Google’s processing is governed by its own policies, including the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you use a version or purchase option that removes ads, Max Player does not initialize the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

5. Network access and notifications

The app uses network access to play streaming content you choose and to load ads in the free version. It may request notification permission to show playback controls while audio or video plays in the background. You can manage these permissions in Android settings.

6. Backup and transfer

If Android backup or device-transfer features are enabled on your device, Android may include Max Player’s local database and preferences in a backup or transfer. This can include the locally stored playback information described above. Backup handling is controlled by your device settings and your Google account or device provider.

7. Data sharing

Max Player does not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising and direct streaming disclosures above, the app does not send your locally stored media, history, favorites, bookmarks, or preferences to the developer or to third parties.

8. Children’s privacy

Max Player is not directed to children under the age at which parental consent is required in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact the developer through the app’s Google Play listing.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest version. Continued use of Max Player after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through the contact details listed on Max Player’s Google Play listing.

Note for the app publisher: Before publishing, replace this contact method with a monitored support email or privacy contact URL if one is available.
